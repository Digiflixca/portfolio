AI Search Visibility

Be the answer AI gives about you

Customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity instead of typing keywords. If your business information is thin or inconsistent, AI fills the gaps — sometimes with fiction.

  • AI Visibility Audit — brand clarity, entity consistency, content authority, structured data, local information, reviews and citation signals.
  • Clear answers — content structured so both people and machines understand what you do.
  • First-hand expertise — real experience and evidence, the kind of content AI systems prefer to cite.
  • Consistent identity — the same name, facts and story everywhere you appear.
Our honest position: AI search is an extension of search strategy, not a magical replacement for SEO. No agency — us included — can promise that ChatGPT, Google, Gemini or Perplexity will include, cite, rank or recommend your business. Anyone who guarantees it is selling you something. What we can do is make your business unmistakably clear, consistent and citable everywhere these systems look.
Local Discovery

Own your neighbourhood on Google

For local businesses, the Google Business Profile and the Map pack decide who gets the call. Most profiles we see are half-finished — wrong hours, no photos, unanswered reviews.

  • Google Business Profile — complete setup, categories, services, photos and posts.
  • Maps visibility — show up where nearby customers are actually looking.
  • Reviews — a steady, honest review engine and professional responses.
  • Local pages & citations — consistent name, address, phone, hours and services everywhere.
A note on access: we only ever work on business profiles with the owner's authorized access — we never claim access to private profile data without it.
